Kozica map data

The .pmtiles files, trails.network (the network of Trails and Bike routes the
app plans Routes over), trails.edges (each Trail's and Bike route's walk along
it) and places.sqlite (the names, the Trails and the Bike routes it searches)
attached to this release are a derivative database of OpenStreetMap.

They were built by scripts/build-map-data.py at the extracts pinned in
scripts/osm-data.pin, both at this release's tag.

by it.
